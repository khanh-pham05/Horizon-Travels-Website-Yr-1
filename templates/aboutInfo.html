Privacy Policy

By creating an account with Horizon Travels, you are agreeing to the collection and usage of your personal data. A full description of how your data is collected and used can be found below.

What personal data do we collect?
We collect data that you have entered to create your account and when booking flights like your email address, name, card details, etc.

Usage of your personal data
Collected data is used for the following purposes:
- For the creation and administration of your account with our services
- To process any payments for bookings
- For effective communication and timely response to your inquiries or requests

Access to your personal data
In accordance with the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of any personal data we hold about you. To submit a request, please email us at personal-data-requests@horizontravels.com.

Where is the data stored and how long for?
All collected personal information is securely stored within our databases and retained only for as long as necessary to provide the services you have requested.

How is your personal data protected?
Sensitive personal information is securely stored within our databases. Whenever possible, data that does not require storage, such as bank card numbers, is processed once and not retained. Additionally, passwords are hashed, ensuring they cannot be stolen or reused, further enhancing the security of your account and personal details.
